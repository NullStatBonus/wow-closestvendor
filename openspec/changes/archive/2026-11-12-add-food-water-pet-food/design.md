# Design

## Context

See proposal.md. Reagents are the model: `Reagents.lua` holds permanent bit numbers, `Watched` gates by known spell, vendor records carry a reagent mask as field 9 (the world coordinates `wx, wy` are appended in memory at 9 and 10, and the runtime reagent mask sits at index 11), and `Triggers` takes a list of low reagent item ids next to the ammo count. The merchant window is scanned once (`merchantScan` in `Adapter.lua`) and the result is stored by `Discovery.Record`. Reagent buying is a separate follow-on feature and is not extended here.

Supplies differ from reagents in three ways: nothing is gated by a spell (food) or the gate is the class or the pet (water, pet food); the set of countable items is large (hundreds of ids, not 16); and the "item" a vendor trip serves is a category, not one item id.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**
- Watch food, water and pet food with separate thresholds and switches, and ask for the nearest seller.
- Count every matching item in the bags, from one generated table, with no level tiers.
- A notice-only outcome for mages (conjure) and warlocks (healthstone).
- Reuse the question, the two-target choice, automatic mode and the notice unchanged.
- Show food, water and pet food in the status window, and play a chosen sound when a reminder shows.

**Non-Goals:**
- Buying food, water or pet food at a merchant.
- A healthstone row in the status window. The display-only Conjured Food and Water rows stay as they are, next to the new Food and Water rows.
- Potions, bandages, soulstones, rogue poisons.
- A per-item switch (each category has one switch).

## Decisions

### 1. A category key, not an item id
A supply request is identified by a key: `"food"`, `"water"` or `"pet"`. The trigger keeps a handled-until-cleared set per key, as it does per reagent id. A vendor trip needs only the key, which `Search` turns into a vendor test on the supply mask. `pet` passes with the pet's current diets, so a vendor qualifies when it sells any of them.

### 2. One generated table, one pure module
`ClosestVendor/Data/Supplies.lua` (generated by `tools/build_supplies.py`, never edited) maps item id to one item mask: bit 0 food, bit 1 drink, bits 2 to 7 the pet diets, bit 8 healthstone. Food and drink come from the client data (an item whose use spell is named "Food" or "Drink"; Wago ItemEffect and SpellName, Era and Forever unioned). Pet diets of items come from the client data too: `Item.ItemPetFoodID` (names in `ItemPetFood`) in the WoW Forever tables, where the Era client has no such column but the item ids are the same. The diets of each pet family come from `CreatureFamily.PetFoodMask`. `Supplies.lua` holds the pure logic: `Counts(itemCounts)`, which turns bag counts into `{ food, water, diets }`, the watched list for a class and pet, the low list, and the key-to-mask mapping. It has no game globals and is tested under `luajit tests/run.lua`.

An item can be both food and pet food (bread), so the table stores a kind and a diet separately. An item that is both food and drink, if any exists in the data, is counted for both; the data spike (task 1.1) decides whether that case occurs.

### 3. Supply mask on the vendor record
A new tenth stored field `supplies` holds eight bits: 0 food, 1 water, 2 to 7 the diets in the game's order (Meat, Fish, Bread, Cheese, Fruit, Fungus). It is separate from the reagent mask so the permanent reagent bit numbers are untouched. `build_vendors.py` writes it from the table and the pfQuest sold-by lists; the merchant scan computes it from the offered items; `Discovery.Record` stores and compares it, defaulting to 0 for old records. The in-memory layout shifts by one: `Core.rebuildBuckets` copies the new field to the runtime index after the reagent mask. The bit numbers are permanent.

### 4. Counting bags
`Adapter` gets a single bag pass that returns counts by item id for the ids in the table, or nil when bags cannot be read (unknown is nil, never 0). `Supplies.Counts` turns that into category totals. Healthstones are counted the same way. No other module calls a container function.

### 5. Pet diet from the game
`Adapter.petDiet()` identifies the active pet by its creature family id, the number `UnitCreatureFamily("pet")` returns after the name (checked in game on Classic Era and WoW Forever: `"Bear", 4`), and maps it to a set of diet names through the generated table `ns.PetFamilies` (family id to diet bits, from the client's `CreatureFamily.PetFoodMask`, Era and Forever unioned). It returns nil when there is no pet, the call is missing or fails, there is no id, or the family is unknown or eats nothing. No localized text is read or matched at runtime, so pet food works in every client language. `GetPetFoodTypes` and `C_PetInfo.GetPetFoodTypes` are not used: the game's names are localized, and neither returned anything in the in-game check. A nil diet watches nothing.

### 6. The remind outcome
`Triggers.Update` gains a `supplies` argument (list of low keys) and `reminders` (list of keys to remind) and may return `{ "remind", kind, key }`. A reminder is not an active request: it never sets `active`, never clears or replaces a pin, and is recorded in a handled set that clears when the condition clears. In combat or an instance nothing is returned and the set is not marked, so the reminder shows after combat. `Controller` prints the chat line and shows the notice, and never calls `findVendor`.

A mage gets `remind` for food or water when the matching conjure spell is known (spell ids come from the existing display-only entries in `Reagents.lua`), and a `go` otherwise. The warlock reminder is a separate key with its own threshold of none carried.

### 7. Settings
`Core` defaults: `foodEnabled`, `waterEnabled`, `petFoodEnabled`, `healthstoneRemind` (all true), `foodThreshold`, `waterThreshold`, `petFoodThreshold` (all 5, range 1 to 40). The master `ammoEnabled` switch gates all of them, so its label stays "Ammo and reagents" (a rename would touch the locale and every spec that names it; noted as a follow-up). A new "Supplies" options page uses the standard checkbox and slider controls already used on the Reagents page, with the water and pet food rows hidden for classes that do not use them.

### 8. Status window rows
One new group `supplies` (after Reagents) holds the rows `supply:food`, `supply:water` and `supply:pet`, with one mode dropdown like the other groups. `Status.Snapshot` reads the bag totals through the same `supplyCounts` and `petDiet` reads, and skips them when the group is `never`. A row is low when `Supplies.Evaluate` puts its key in `go` or `remind`, so a row and its warning cannot disagree; the master switch applies as for reagents. Water shows for mana classes only, pet food for a hunter with a known family only, and an unknown count has no row. A click on a row pins the nearest seller of that supply without a question, as for any routable row. `UNIT_PET` also redraws the window.

### 9. Reminder sound
`Supplies.SOUNDS` lists the choices as stable keys with a game sound id: `whisper` (default), `ping`, `invite`, `ready` and `none`. The saved setting is the key, never the id, and `none` is the off switch. `Adapter.playSound(id)` is the only call to the game, guarded in `pcall`. `Controller` plays the chosen sound once per batch of reminders (food and water on the same tick make one sound). `/cvn test remind` goes through the same path. Choosing a sound on the settings page or with `/cvn sound <key>` plays it as a preview. A "map pin" choice is not offered: the pin's own sound comes from the game or from TomTom and no sound id for it is known. The ids are checked in game.

### 10. Strings
Every sentence is one `L["..."]` with `%s` and `%d` placeholders: the question and notice for a supply, the "no known vendor" lines, the reminder lines, the command replies. Nothing is built by concatenation.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- **Diet data (resolved, client data).** The WoW Forever `Item` table carries `ItemPetFoodID`, a link to `ItemPetFood` (Meat, Fish, Cheese, Bread, Fungus, Fruit, and the removed Raw Meat and Raw Fish). That is the item diet source, so no server database, wiki or addon data is used and no third-party notice is needed. It lists 384 items (the raw types and rows without an item name are left out). Compared with two outside lists (LibPeriodicTable and the earlier server-database table), 176 items agree and 9 are given a different diet by the client, mostly newer re-categorisations (for example omelets are Meat in the client and Cheese elsewhere); these 9 need an in-game check. A few items other lists give a diet (for example Plainstrider Meat, Gingerbread Cookie) have none in the client data and are not counted. The Era client has no diet column, so an Era-only difference from Forever cannot be seen in the data.
- **Pet family id (resolved).** `UnitCreatureFamily` returns the family id on both clients, so pet food needs no text and no per-flavor call. Families that eat nothing (for example a pet added by a later patch that this table does not know) watch nothing, never the wrong food. The table is generated, so a new patch needs a rerun of `tools/build_supplies.py`.
- **Forever item links.** Forever's `ItemEffect` has no item column; `tools/fetch_data.py` also fetches its `ItemXItemEffect` link table.
- **Meat and fish have few vendors.** A meat-only pet often has no known seller; the addon says so once. This is expected, not a bug.
- **Table size.** A few hundred to a thousand entries load once at startup. Negligible, and the file is generated.
- **Flavor differences.** The Vanilla and Camelot builds can know different items. The table holds the union, since an id the client does not know is never counted.
- **Rank and class gates.** Water for hunters, druids and paladins assumes they all use mana in these clients; task 1.3 confirms it from the class data.
- **Index shift.** Adding a stored field moves runtime indices; `tests/test_search.lua` and `tests/test_discovery.lua` must cover old 9-field and new 10-field records.

## Migration

Saved learned vendors have no `supplies` field and read as 0 until the merchant is opened again, which updates them. New settings take their defaults when absent. Nothing saved is removed or renamed.
